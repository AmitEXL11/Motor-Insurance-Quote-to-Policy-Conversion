The Problem
Where quotes die before becoming policies
Motor insurers lose a significant share of quotes across four friction points before a policy is ever issued.
💸
Price Sensitivity
38% of abandonments cite premium too high vs. peer segment. Aggregator comparison happens in real time.
📄
Document Friction
24% drop off at document upload. RC book file size limits and unclear licence scans are the top causes.
⏳
Underwriting Delays
Manual review takes 28+ minutes on average. 12.4% of cases breach the 48-hour SLA, leading to lapse.
📡
Channel Drop-off
Aggregator-sourced quotes convert at 8.4% vs 18.2% direct. No re-engagement after hand-off.
